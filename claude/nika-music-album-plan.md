## Rules
- All new songs made on Suno Pro (an active paid plan is needed for commercial rights; subscribing doesn't grant rights to free-plan songs retroactively).
- No real players' faces or likeness. Supporters are the main characters; players only as small silhouettes from behind, or left out entirely (JUARA! had none and didn't need them). No names or numbers on players' shirts.
- No PSSI crest, no FIFA/AFC logos, no sponsor brands. The son's replica jersey keeps its pattern but never the crest or the maker's logo: check every image for a crest (A6 of JUARA! came back with one and had to be edited).
- The Garuda is always a stylised mythical eagle, never the national coat of arms (no shield, no emblem).
- Don't delete the old videos. Old free-plan songs keep monetization off once YPP returns.
- **Visual look for the whole album (decided 9 Oct): illustrated Indonesian poster / street-mural style**, the one used for JUARA!. The photoreal Veo video was stopped: next to the real final it read as a poor imitation, and Veo struggled with crowds and stadiums. Each song gets an illustrated lyric video (8–10 artworks, Veo motion only on the instrumentals). An animated music video is made only for a song that clearly performs.

## Language & sound
- Bahasa Indonesia first. Chorus = short chant (2–4 words, call-and-response), verses = plain concrete everyday images (warung TV, syal, Bapak) in the style of current Indonesian indie/pop hits (Hindia, Nadhif Basalamah, Tulus, Perunggu, Last Child). Avoid slogan-only lines.
- English only as a hook word ("Garuda Calling") or as a separate version of a flagship (Garuda Calling: Reborn gets both).
- Two fixed voices: MALE lead = gritty, raspy baritone-tenor, punchy rhythmic verses, shouted anthemic belts. FEMALE counter-voice = warm operatic mezzo-soprano for intros, bridges and final lifts, and lead on the sad tracks (#1, #2). Artist names can't go in Suno prompts, so describe the traits only.
- **Base style string = the one that made JUARA! (proven):**
  ```
  Arena rock with arena-sized reverb tails; recurring brass, distorted guitar, and angklung riff over orchestral strings, piano, choir, stomp-claps, and taiko; gritty, raspy male baritone-tenor lead with warm operatic mezzo-soprano counter-vocal; driving 128 bpm, singable anthem with a key-change finale
  ```
  Per track, change only the tempo and the mood words (e.g. ballad: "slow 72 bpm, piano and strings lead, mezzo-soprano lead vocal").
- No Persona (not available in Rawa's Suno, checked 10 Oct): the vocal is locked by pasting the same style string on every track; only the tempo and mood words change.
- Garuda Calling: Reborn = same lyrics, fresh Pro generation (no Cover, Remaster or Inspo of the free-plan song). It will have a new melody: the "Reborn" title frames that.

## Song production rules (learned on JUARA!)
- **Length 3:30–4:30.** JUARA! ran 5:57 with a 49 s instrumental right after the hook (0:35–1:24) and another 32 s break (3:54–4:26); both needed extra Veo clips to keep viewers. Use at most one short instrumental intro (≤15 s) and one break (≤20 s); avoid stacking [Instrumental …] tags.
- Hook in the first 20 seconds (JUARA!: first chant at 0:22).
- Forcing the instrumental riff to play the chorus melody did not work in Suno; use normal structure tags.
- Pronunciation: spell sounds phonetically in the Suno lyrics box and put "Indonesian" in the tag; keep normal spelling on screen and in titles. Abbreviations always as Indonesian letter sounds: GBK → Ge-Be-Ka, PSSI → Pe-Es-Es-I, AFC → A-Ef-Ce, TV → Ti-Vi. "Ayo" at the start of a song or section → Ah-yo.
- Fix single bad sections with Replace Section instead of rerolling the whole song.
- Model: v6 mini on Pro gave the best verse delivery.
- Export from Suno: the MP3 plus the SRT and LRC with word timings (the LRC drives the karaoke).

## Video production (pipeline built on JUARA!)
- **Artworks (Gemini, one model for the whole album):** reuse the JUARA! character sheet and A1 as the style reference; each prompt starts with a code (A1…, M1-IMG, M1-VEO) and the files are named by code. Download images at full size (2752×1536): A2, A3 and A7 of JUARA! came in at half size and look softer in the Shorts.
- Prompts ask for a "simple, uncluttered lower middle" and forbid drawn bands or overlays (Gemini drew a dark bar when asked for a "darker band"). The text backing is added in the edit.
- **Motion (Veo):** only on the instrumentals, a different 10 s clip every ~10 s, never a loop. Close-ups are redrawn by Gemini from the artwork first; Veo can't crop. If Veo refuses ("I can't generate that video"): retry once, then keep the camera still, then simplify the first frame (fewer people, no smoke), last resort a still with a slow zoom.
- **Edit:** `edit/render.py` (16:9 lyric video) and `edit/shorts.py` (six 9:16 Shorts) render from the artworks, clips, song and LRC: karaoke lyrics in Anton, beat pulses and flashes from the word times, rain/confetti overlays, the end-screen message in Bahasa Indonesia. Before the next song, the JUARA!-specific timeline moves into a per-song config so the same scripts render every track.
- **Hand-over:** Rawa uploads assets through GitHub (≤25 MB per file); the finished 1080p video comes back as HEVC under 100 MB in `release/`, Shorts under 30 MB directly in the chat.
