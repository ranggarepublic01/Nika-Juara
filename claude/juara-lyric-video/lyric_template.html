Art direction

The same father-and-son story as the paused photoreal video, told in ten illustrated posters instead of footage. A stylised world isn't compared with the real final at GBK, and image models keep a flat poster style far more consistent than photoreal crowds and stadiums. The big lyrics do the work; the artworks carry the story under them.

The look is Indonesian screen-printed poster and street mural: flat shapes, hand-cut edges, halftone and paper grain, one strict palette. The two warung flashback artworks drop to cold blue-grey with the scarf as the only red, the same rule as before.

Rules

  • No players at all. The supporters carry the whole video, so no silhouette can point at a real player, and there is no trophy to get wrong.
  • No logos anywhere: no PSSI crest, no FIFA/AFC marks, no sponsors. Flags are plain red over white.
  • Garuda is a mythical eagle, not the coat of arms: no shield, no emblem, no banner (A1).
  • Only two pieces of lettering: INDONESIA on the scarf and 12 on the son's jersey. Check both in every image. The illustrated son wears the plain red jersey with the 12, which settles the open jersey question for this video.
  • The scarf travels: in Bapak's hands a year ago (A4), with the son at GBK (A2, A3, A5, A6, A8), around Bapak's neck from A9 to the end. In A7 Bapak has no scarf.
  • Screens never face the camera. The warung is always seen from beside the TV.
  • Motorbike riders wear helmets (A8).
  • Leave room for the words: every prompt keeps the subject in the centre third and a calm band across the lower middle. The centre-third rule also makes 9:16 crops for Shorts work.

GBK references

Two real photos are used for the architecture only. The prompts tell Gemini to copy the structure and redraw it at night in the poster style.

  • GBK photo 1 (aerial, outside): the daytime aerial with the Sudirman towers directly behind the stadium. Attach to A1 and A3.
  • GBK photo 2 (inside the bowl): the view across the pitch from the stands, with the red track and the roof trusses. Attach to A2.
  • What makes it read as GBK: outside, one ring roof of radial ribbed panels with a thin outer edge and an open oval centre, a facade of white columns with V-shaped braces, and the tower cluster right behind. Inside, red, white and grey seat blocks in a flag pattern, a red-orange running track around the pitch, and radial trusses under the roof. Check these in A1, A2 and A3.
  • A5 and A6 keep the stadium as a soft abstract background, so they don't need a photo.

Workflow

  1. A1 with GBK photo 1 attached. This is the style anchor: iterate on it until the style is what you want, because every other artwork copies it.
  2. Pick one image model and keep it for all ten artworks and the character sheet. GPT and Gemini draw the same prompt in clearly different styles, so mixing them breaks the set.
  3. Character sheet with A1 attached as the style reference. Regenerate until the son, Bapak and the scarf look right, with INDONESIA spelled correctly.
  4. A2–A10: attach the character sheet first, then A1 as the style reference, then the GBK photo where the card lists one (A2: photo 2, A3: photo 1). For the present-day warung artworks (A7, A9), also attach A4 so the room stays the same.
  5. Check every image for spelling of INDONESIA, a 12 only on the son, stray text, an emblem on the Garuda, helmets, and the GBK details listed under GBK references.
  6. Upscale every artwork to 3840×2160 before editing. The edit zooms up to 140%, and a Gemini-size image goes soft at that scale.
  7. Optional motion: any artwork can become a Veo clip with its motion prompt (first frame = the artwork). Start with the longest holds: A3 (49s), A8 (43s), A10 (59s). Mute the Veo audio.
  8. Edit on the Suno track from 0:00.0 using the timeline and lyric spec below. Captions come from lyrics/JUARA_lyric-video.srt.

Over the whole video

  • Text over busy art: where a line sits over detail (the A1 facade, crowds), put a soft charcoal gradient behind the lower band, about 60% at the bottom fading to 0, instead of shrinking the text.
  • Paper grain: one grain or paper-texture overlay across the whole video at 10–15% opacity, so the artworks and the text read as one print.
  • Rain overlay on both A4 stretches. Confetti or sparks on the chorus stretches. Light flares on floodlights and fireworks.
  • Audio: the Suno track only, full volume. No sound effects.

Building it in CapCut

  • Import lyrics/JUARA_lyric-video.srt as captions. It has one caption per sung line with clean in and out times, and no line hangs over an instrumental.
  • Verse, chorus-line and bridge captions: apply the style above and a karaoke text animation stretched over the caption's length.
  • Chant, JUARA and Asia lines: replace the caption with one text layer per word, placed on the word times in the timing table below.
  • YouTube: upload the same SRT as Indonesian subtitles. The original Suno SRT keeps "Garuda di dada, kita juara!" on screen from 0:33 to 1:24 and holds other lines through instrumentals.
  • Shorts: 2:12.2–2:55.6 (first chorus and chant, 43s) and 4:40.2–5:12.6 (the scarf comes home into the final chorus, 32s) crop to 9:16 cleanly.
