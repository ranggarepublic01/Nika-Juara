Art direction

The same father-and-son story as the paused photoreal video, told in ten illustrated posters instead of footage. A stylised world isn't compared with the real final at GBK, and image models keep a flat poster style far more consistent than photoreal crowds and stadiums. The big lyrics do the work; the artworks carry the story under them.

The look is Indonesian screen-printed poster and street mural: flat shapes, hand-cut edges, halftone and paper grain, one strict palette. The two warung flashback artworks drop to cold blue-grey with the scarf as the only red, the same rule as before.

Rules

  • No players at all. The supporters carry the whole video, so no silhouette can point at a real player, and there is no trophy to get wrong.
  • No logos anywhere: no PSSI crest, no FIFA/AFC marks, no sponsors. Flags are plain red over white.
  • Garuda is a mythical eagle, not the coat of arms: no shield, no emblem, no banner (A1).
  • Only two pieces of lettering: INDONESIA on the scarf and 12 on the son's jersey. Check both in every image. The illustrated son wears the plain red jersey with the 12, which settles the open jersey question for this video.
  • The scarf travels: in Bapak's hands a year ago (A4), with the son at GBK (A2, A3, A5, A6, A8), around Bapak's neck from A9 to the end. In A7 Bapak has no scarf.
  • Screens never face the camera. The warung is always seen from beside the TV.
  • Motorbike riders wear helmets (A8).
  • Leave room for the words: every prompt keeps the subject in the centre third and a calm band across the lower middle. The centre-third rule also makes 9:16 crops for Shorts work.
Style line (already inside every prompt)
Bold Indonesian screen-printed poster and hand-painted street-mural illustration: flat simplified shapes with confident hand-cut edges, a strict limited palette of flag red, warm off-white, deep night indigo, charcoal black and small touches of gold, visible halftone dots, dry-brush texture and rough paper grain, light drawn as flat bright shapes and rays, strong silhouettes, people drawn with simplified but expressive faces. Not photorealistic, not a 3D render, not anime. Landscape 16:9. Do not draw any words, titles, lyrics, numbers, logos, crests, badges, emblems, watermarks or captions; the only lettering allowed is the word INDONESIA on the scarf and the number 12 on the son's jersey.
Composition line (already inside every prompt)
Keep the main subject inside the centre third of the width. Keep a calm, darker band across the lower middle of the frame (from about 55% to 85% of the height) with no faces or important detail, for big lyric text.

Workflow

  1. Character sheet (attach nothing). Regenerate until the son, Bapak and the scarf look right, with INDONESIA spelled correctly.
  2. A1 with your real GBK photo attached. This is the style anchor: iterate on it until the style is what you want, because every other artwork copies it.
  3. A2–A10: attach the character sheet first, then A1 as the style reference. For the present-day warung artworks (A7, A9), also attach A4 so the room stays the same.
  4. Check every image for spelling of INDONESIA, a 12 only on the son, stray text, an emblem on the Garuda, and helmets.
  5. Upscale every artwork to 3840×2160 before editing. The edit zooms up to 140%, and a Gemini-size image goes soft at that scale.
  6. Optional motion: any artwork can become a Veo clip with its motion prompt (first frame = the artwork). Start with the longest holds: A3 (49s), A8 (43s), A10 (59s). Mute the Veo audio.
  7. Edit on the Suno track from 0:00.0 using the timeline and lyric spec below. Captions come from lyrics/JUARA_lyric-video.srt.
