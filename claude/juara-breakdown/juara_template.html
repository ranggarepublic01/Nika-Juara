Story

A father and son, one red scarf, two places on the same night. A year ago at the neighbourhood warung, they switched off the TV after the World Cup dream ended, and Bapak folded his scarf and said "nanti juga menang". Tonight the son carries that scarf into a packed GBK for the final, while Bapak watches at the same warung with the neighbours. After the win the son rides home and wraps the scarf around his father's neck. The video ends at dawn, looking toward Asia.

Look rules

Present night
Warm, saturated, red and white, floodlights. The son wears the plain red jersey.
One year earlier
Cold blue-grey, heavy rain. The son wears a grey T-shirt. The scarf is the only red.
Dawn
Soft gold light for the last three scenes.
  • No real players. Players appear only from behind or as backlit silhouettes, and are never close enough to show a face.
  • No logos anywhere: no PSSI crest, no FIFA/AFC marks, no sponsors. The trophy is a generic gold cup.
  • Only two pieces of lettering: INDONESIA on the scarf and the number 12 on the son's jersey (12 = the supporters, "pemain ke-12"). Players on the pitch keep plain kits with no names or numbers, so no silhouette points at a real player. Check the spelling of INDONESIA and the 12 in every image.
  • The scarf travels: in Bapak's hands in the flashback, around the son's neck at GBK, around Bapak's neck from Sc33 to the end.
  • Phones and TVs never show their screens to the camera. The camera sits beside the TV (warung View B) or behind a phone, so we see faces lit by the glow.
  • Nobody sings or lip-syncs. Lines are carried by action: the chest thump on "Garuda di dada", the phone on "Bilang ke Bapak", the smile on "Bapak tersenyum".
  • Motorbike riders wear helmets.
  • Reuse earlier first frames as references. A scene that returns to a setup already made (same place, same people) lists that scene's first-frame image under "Also attach". Rawa found this on Sc21, made from the Sc11-A image: it keeps faces, light and the room consistent better than the plates alone.
  • Crowds and stadium architecture are the image model's weak spot. Face-on shots of the son in the stands are framed tight on a long lens, so the stadium behind him is a soft red-and-white blur. The stadium itself is shown only in the wide shots that use the GBK plates.
  • Veo invents what it cannot see. Big camera moves (rising, tilting up, reveals) make it build new roof or stands. Every video prompt now ends with a lock line (one continuous shot, setting exactly as in the first frame). If a clip still grows new architecture, regenerate with the camera move removed: "the camera holds still".
  • Every image prompt starts with its scene tag. If the image model draws the tag as text, delete it from that prompt and regenerate.

Reference images

Make these before any scene. Attach the character sheet and the scene's plate to every first-frame image. If a scene drifts, regenerate with the plate attached first.

For the GBK plates (P1–P6), also attach a real photo of the stadium as a visual reference: one of your own, or a freely licensed one (Wikimedia Commons has several). The ring roof is what makes it read as GBK; check it on every plate before you use it. Generate P1 first, then the other GBK views with P1 attached ("the same stadium as the attached image, seen from …").
